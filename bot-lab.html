How to read this report

The built-in robot does not use an external AI service. A low score is not proof that advanced AI bots cannot solve the challenge. Image-recognition resistance is not measured in this first version.

Safety limits: up to 6 challenge requests per run; only the selected site; no Secret Key is read or sent. Tests use real BeeCaptcha endpoints and your signed-in dashboard session.
