Waiting for test results

The AI model can detect common objects, but it is only one bot strategy and a small sample is not a certification. A successful AI solve is a concrete weakness; a failed solve is not proof that other models cannot solve it.

Safety limits: maximum 6 live challenge requests per run; only the selected site; no Secret Key is read; wrong answers are tested on a fresh challenge. A separate authenticated server-side suite creates and removes temporary fixture rows to test replay, expiry, invalid tokens, and attempt limits. The browser downloads pinned model scripts and model weights; image inference runs locally, not through an AI inference API.
